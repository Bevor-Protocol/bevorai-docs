# Integrating Into Protocols & Chains

Integrate Bevor directly into your protocol's architecture or blockchain infrastructure. Create seamless security experiences for your users, automate protocol-specific security checks, and build security directly into your protocol's DNA.

### Custom protocol tooling

Use the planned SDK or API to build protocol-specific security workflows around your own requirements. This can include specialized monitoring, ecosystem reporting, or builder tooling, but those are custom solutions rather than core Bevor product surfaces.

Provide security context directly to builders in your ecosystem through tools you control. Whether you're building a new chain or protocol, the shared graph can underpin specialized security experiences without splitting findings into another system.

We actively sponsor teams building innovative security solutions - reach out to us to discuss trial access and potential sponsorship opportunities for builders in your ecosystem.

<Note>
Building a new protocol or chain? Email [contact@bevor.io](mailto:contact@bevor.io) to request support for your smart contract language or to get trial access for teams building in your ecosystem.
</Note>
