Use privacy-preserving features for sensitive client engagements.

## On-premise deployment

For regulated or air‑gapped environments, we offer on‑premise deployments of BevorAI. Contact us to discuss an enterprise plan: <a href="mailto:contact@bevor.io">contact@bevor.io</a>.

## Controls

- Local-only analysis options
- Redaction of secrets and identifiers
- Granular data retention controls
- Access controls and audit logs

## Recommendations

1. Enable privacy mode in Terminal
2. Restrict data sharing and telemetry
3. Use redaction for reports and exports
4. Periodically review access logs
