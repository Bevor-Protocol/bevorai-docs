# Integrating Into Protocols & Chains

Integrate BevorAI directly into your protocol's architecture or blockchain infrastructure. Create seamless security experiences for your users, automate protocol-specific security checks, and build security directly into your protocol's DNA.

### Network-Wide Monitoring
Monitor activity across your entire protocol ecosystem with custom integrations. Our [Network-Level Monitoring](/developers/network-monitoring) tools help you track security patterns, detect threats, and analyze risk across multiple chains and deployments.

Want to build custom monitoring solutions? Check out our [API Reference](/api-reference/introduction) for integration endpoints, or reach out to our team to discuss your specific needs.

### Smart Contract Integration / Protocol Level Monitoring
Provide security tooling directly to builders in your ecosystem through custom-built solutions using our API. Whether you're building a new chain or protocol, we can help you create specialized security tools for your platform.

We actively sponsor teams building innovative security solutions - reach out to us to discuss trial access and potential sponsorship opportunities for builders in your ecosystem.

<Note>
Building a new protocol or chain? Email [contact@bevor.io](mailto:contact@bevor.io) to request support for your smart contract language or to get trial access for teams building in your ecosystem.
</Note>
